A little about how I think.

I study Information Science, so I like understanding how things work. I’m drawn to product management because the interesting questions sit with people: what they notice, what they ignore, and why they choose one thing over another.

Growing an Instagram audience from scratch taught me about attention, preferences, experimentation and engagement. I checked what people responded to, tried something different, and measured again. That is the same loop I want to bring to product discovery and user-centred decisions. I haven’t held a Product Manager role yet. My product experience comes from self-directed case studies.
